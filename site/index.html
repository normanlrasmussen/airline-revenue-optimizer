AIRLINE REVENUE MANAGEMENT

Which bookings should an airline accept?

AeroYield is an operations-research portfolio project that combines U.S. airline market data with simulated booking demand to compare seat-control policies. The core decision is simple: sell a seat now, or protect it for a customer who may pay more later.
