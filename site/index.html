AIRLINE REVENUE MANAGEMENT

Which bookings should an airline accept?

AeroYield uses U.S. airline market data, simulated booking demand, and optimization to test how seat-control decisions change flight revenue. The core trade-off is simple: sell a seat now, or save it for a customer who may pay more later.
