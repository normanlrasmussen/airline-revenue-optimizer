METHOD

A deliberately simple optimization core.

01

Observe

Use DB1C fare, passenger, carrier, origin, and destination data to characterize route economics.

02

Forecast

For the MVP, demand is an editable point forecast. Later versions can estimate class-specific distributions.

03

Optimize

Choose integer seats by fare class to maximize modeled ticket revenue subject to capacity and demand limits.

maximize Σ fare[k] × seats[k] subject to Σ seats[k] ≤ capacity 0 ≤ seats[k] ≤ forecast_demand[k], integer

Next math: Littlewood → EMSR-b → overbooking → stochastic demand → bid prices → network revenue management.
