BOOKING SIMULATOR

Compare seat-control policies on the same uncertain demand.

Each replication generates one booking stream and gives that exact stream to every policy. Repeating the experiment isolates policy quality from random luck and shows the revenue distribution, not just one outcome.

Common random numbers: Open Sales, EMSR-b, Dynamic Programming, and the clairvoyant benchmark all see identical requests within each replication. Differences in results therefore come from the policy, not different simulated customers.
